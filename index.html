The Story

When Ruin Answers First

In the town of Mastic Harbor, the rain does not simply fall. It hides things. It carries sirens through empty streets. It turns broken glass into glitter and blood into memory.

Asher has just asked Sara to marry him. For one impossible moment, the future feels close enough to touch. Then a dark sedan tears through the night, and everything he believed was waiting for him is left bleeding beneath the storm.

On that same night, Freya dies and falls into Hell. Their paths do not cross yet, but fate has already put its hands on both of them. This is Asher’s story before Redemption. Before Freya. Before the truth. Before dawn.

The World Mastic Harbor

A rain-streaked town of old money, quiet streets, neon diner signs, police lights, and secrets buried under polished lawns. Mastic Harbor is not real, but the grief inside it is.

The Call Excerpt

The rain had started before midnight and never really stopped.

It filled the streets with puddles and drummed steadily against rooftops and pavement alike. In the last hour it had grown heavier, turning the night into a dark curtain of water. The dark skies and relentless rain felt ominous. Like the universe itself was issuing some kind of warning.

Officer Daniel Hargrove tapped the steering wheel of his patrol car as the wipers pushed sheets of water across the windshield in slow, steady arcs.

The radio crackled. “Unit twelve respond to a possible home invasion. Caller reports sounds of breaking glass and yelling. Address is fourteen eighty-two Willow Crest.”

Hargrove sighed. “Well here we go. Unit twelve responding.”
